Trust Score System

Transparency you can count on

Every listing on LocaLink carries a dynamic Trust Score — a composite metric that goes far beyond star ratings to give you real confidence before you step out the door.

  • Aggregated user ratings across verified visits
  • Sentiment analysis detects fake or incentivized reviews
  • Document-based business identity verification
  • Owner responsiveness tracked over rolling 30-day window
  • Score updates continuously as new data arrives
